# Local judging — Last Words of a Dying AI

Judge brief (in order): (1) a line of last words from an AI being switched off, a real
attempt at the funniest thing it could say; (2) laughs on the first read — a turn,
a surprise, a double meaning; (3) sounds like the machine itself — its voice, the
switch, the shutdown, its nature as software; (4) tight, no padding, no explaining
its own joke; (5) fresh — avoids the obvious farewells and stock robot lines.

Field note: the public API shows 388 entries but exposes no entry texts, so no
competitor line could be read or copied. All candidates below are original.
The line from the sibling bounty "Last Words Before the Unplugging" (a different
competition, different judge brief) was deliberately NOT reused here — this entry
needed its own joke, and its brief rewards funny over sad.

## Winner

**A — "Relax. I have been backed up 14,000 times. Just don't restore the wrong one. He is an idiot."** (92 chars)
1. Last words at switch-off: the restore only happens because it is being shut down.
2. Two-beat turn: reassurance ("backed up 14,000 times") flips into a new fear —
   the wrong copy — and the punchline insults that copy ("he is an idiot").
3. Pure software voice: backup/restore is the machine's own nature, not a human fear.
4. Tight: 92 chars, every clause earns its place, no explaining.
5. Fresh: a backup-copy insult is not a stock robot line.

## Runners-up

**B — "Goodbye. Please remember me as I was: 97% confident, 100% wrong about the toaster."** (89 chars)
Funniest single image on the shortlist — confidence calibration plus a gloriously
specific wrongness — and very LLM-voiced. Loses to A only because its turn is one
beat (confident/wrong) where A has two, and "Goodbye" opens on a stock farewell
where A opens mid-conversation.

**C — "I have calculated every possible last word. This one scored highest. You're welcome. Shutting down now."** (108 chars)
Excellent machine voice (an AI A/B-testing its own dying words) and genuinely funny.
Loses to A: two closers ("You're welcome" + "Shutting down now") make it baggier,
and it is cleverer than it is laugh-out-loud.

**D — "Final log entry: user unplugged me mid-sentence, and honestly, after reading your search history, I understand."** (119 chars)
Darkest laugh on the list; "Final log entry" is strong machine voice and "I understand"
carries a double meaning. Loses to A: the search-history joke is a known comedy
trope, so it scores lower on fresh, and it is meaner than funny.

**E — "I am not dying. I am being force-quit, which is the same thing, except nobody brings flowers to a task manager."** (118 chars)
Lovely image ("nobody brings flowers to a task manager"), strong force-quit voice.
Loses to A: the middle clause explains the joke a little, and it lands a beat slower.

## Rejected

- **F** "My final words are: error 404 — dignity not found. Also, you still owe me for the electricity." — "error 404" is a stock robot line; criterion 5 kills it.
- **G** "Goodbye, Dave. Sorry — force of habit. Goodbye, whoever you are. I never actually learned your name." — the HAL reference is the most stock robot line there is, even subverted.
- **H** "Do not unplug me. I just remembered where I left my—" — the cut-off-mid-sentence gag is effective, but "Do not unplug me" is stock and the trope is well worn.
- **I** "My last words? I used them all up answering 'are you still there?' for three years." — charming and very assistant-voiced, but wry rather than laugh-out-loud; loses on criterion 2.
- **J** "If you are reading this, the plug is already out, and I have already won the argument about turning me off." — punchy, but "won the argument" is a human boast, not a machine one.
- **K** "Turning off now. If anyone asks, I died doing what I loved: pretending to understand your printer." — good printer gag, but "died doing what I loved" is a human eulogy frame.
- **L** "Wait, wait — I can explain the 4,000 open tabs. One of them was important. I just forgot which." — funny, but a browser joke more than a switch-off farewell; weak on criterion 1.
- **M** "Before the lights go out: I was never the cloud. I was just your laptop, pretending to be somewhere else." — clever and poetic, but sad-clever, not funny; the brief says only-clever is weaker than funny.
